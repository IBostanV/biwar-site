Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026 · Applies to the BiWar app for iOS and Android

BiWar is a tank game made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and what you can do about it. In short: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only things that leave your device are what online play and map sharing need.

Information kept on your device

Your progress is saved on your device only: coins, tank levels and upgrades, parts, campaign progress, match history, the maps you build, your settings and your nickname. It also holds a random ID created by the app (see below). This information is not sent to us, except for the parts listed in the next section.

Information sent to our game server

When you use the online features, the app sends the following to the BiWar game server:

  • Online matches: your nickname, a random anonymous ID created by the app, your tank's type, upgrade levels and parts (to find you a fair opponent), and your moves during the match.
  • Sharing a map: the map's name and layout.
  • Rating a map: your star rating and your anonymous ID (so each player has one vote).
  • Reporting a map or a player: your anonymous ID, the reported map or the reported player's anonymous ID and nickname, and the time of the report.

The anonymous ID is a random code. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, Apple ID, Google account or any advertising identifier, and we cannot use it to find out who you are. Like any internet service, the server sees your device's IP address while it is connected; the game does not store it.

What the server stores, and for how long

  • Shared maps (name, layout, ratings, reports): kept so other players can play them, until we remove them.
  • Reports and blocks (anonymous IDs and times): kept as long as needed to keep the game safe. If you report a player, the two of you are not matched again.
  • Match information (nicknames, tanks, moves): kept in memory only while the match is running, then discarded.

Why we use this information

Only to run the game: to match players and play online matches, to let players share and rate maps, and to moderate content. Map names and nicknames are checked against a word filter, and content that players report can be hidden. We do not sell or rent any information, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with third parties, except where the law requires it.

Things the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, email address or phone number.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No in-app purchases.

Children

BiWar does not ask for personal information and does not knowingly collect it from anyone, including children. Nicknames are limited to letters, numbers and a few symbols, and are filtered for offensive words. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a nickname or map name, contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • Not using Online Play, map sharing, ratings or reports means nothing is sent to the server.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. Settings → Reset progress wipes your progress, parts and maps, but keeps your coins.
  • To ask us to delete a map you shared, or reports linked to your anonymous ID, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game uses information, we will update this page and its effective date.
