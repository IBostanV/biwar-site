Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drive with the joystick on the left and shoot with FIRE. Your energy bar refills over time; every shot uses some. Pick up power-ups on the floor and use them with the buttons above FIRE. In Settings → Controls you can swap FIRE to the left side.

What game modes are there?

  • Online Play: you and a bot teammate against another player and their bot (2 vs 2), or 1 vs 1.
  • Campaign: stages of drone waves, some with bosses; clear every drone to win.
  • Play vs Bots: practice on any map style or on your own maps.

How do I upgrade my tank?

Open Garage. Spend coins on stat upgrades, or open Parts to buy and upgrade parts. The standard cannon can be upgraded too: 50 levels, with a new bonus every 10.

The app closed during a match. Is it lost?

No. Open BiWar again and the main menu offers Resume. Matches against bots and campaign stages continue where you left off. Online matches continue if they are still running; a bot drives your tank while you are away.

Online Play can't find an opponent

Matches pair players with tanks of similar power. If nobody close to your power is searching, the range widens the longer you wait. Make sure you are connected to the internet.

How do I share a map with a friend?

Build it in Play vs Bots → My Maps → New Map, then tap SHARE to get a six-letter code. Your friend adds it with My Maps → Add by code. Popular maps appear in Top Maps.

How do I report a player or a map?

  • A player: after an online match, tap the ⚑ next to their name on the results screen, then tap again to confirm. You won't be matched with them again.
  • A map: tap the ⚑ on its card in Top Maps, or ⚑ Report on the rating card after playing it. It is hidden for you straight away.

Reports are reviewed, and content reported by several players is hidden automatically. You can also email us.

I bought gold but didn't get it

Restart BiWar while connected to the internet: a purchase that was interrupted is credited at the next start. Payments that are still pending (some payment methods take time) are credited once the store confirms them. If the gold still doesn't arrive, email us with the store's receipt. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google: request them through the App Store or Google Play.

Is my gold saved if I delete the app?

No. Your progress, gold included, is stored only on your device, so deleting the app removes it.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset progress wipes your progress, upgrades, parts and maps. Your coins are kept.

Is my data collected?

Very little, and never for ads or tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
